New in this beta

Since the last beta (82d69bb7, 2026-11-07):

  • The picture is drawn on the GPU everywhere: Direct3D 11 on Windows, Metal on Mac and iPhone, OpenGL on Linux and Android, WebGL in the browser. If the picture is wrong or the game will not start, tell us (and say your graphics card).
  • RETRO TV (a mod, off until you turn it on in OPTIONS → MODS): the picture as a TV shows it -- curved glass and a TV signal -- or any RetroArch .slangp shader preset you put in Documents/SWOS/shaders, chosen on its page.
  • PC MENU SHADING (a mod, on by default): the menus' buttons shaded as the PC game shades them. F5 switches between the PC and the Amiga look at any time.
  • MENU BACKGROUNDS (a mod, on by default): a picture of your own behind the menus -- PNG, BMP or JPEG in Documents/SWOS/backgrounds.
  • Chat in an online match: TAB to type, F3 to hide it, 1-0 for quick messages; on a phone, a CHAT button.
  • The mouse in the menus: point to select, click to choose, right click to go back, the wheel to step.
  • OPTIONS → LANGUAGE: the menus in English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano or Nederlands, from the next start.
  • When your game data needs updating the game now says so on its own screen, with LAUNCH SETUP; OPTIONS → SETUP opens OpenSWOS Setup at any time. The setup remembers what you ticked, and ticks what is already installed.
  • A mod's page shows who made it. Hair: bald is now all bald (shaved).
  • Online: a two-legged tie's injuries and sendings-off carry over to the second leg. You can only play someone on this beta.
  • Fixed: saving your options no longer resets the ones you did not change (shaders, for one); a loaded career no longer brings last season's Cup Winners' Cup back; the rain no longer keeps falling over REPLAY / EXIT after a friendly; QUIT THE MATCH? and REPLAY / EXIT are shaded like the other menus.
  • The downloads are smaller (the programs are stripped of their debug information).

And since the last release (c07f39fc, 2026-11-04):

  • Mods. OPTIONS → MODS turns them on and off, and MODES on the main menu holds what they add:
    • PRACTICE: a penalty shootout (with a shooting order and your own keeper), a demo game, team training (lines on or off, the clock stopped, no cards or injuries) and shooting practice.
    • SPEEDRUN: eight targets (a goal, a header, a tackle, three goals...), timed to the frame, your best times kept.
    • CHALLENGES: win from where a match stands -- 1-0 up with 20 minutes to go, ten men, five minutes left. Make your own with NEW CHALLENGE.
  • Historic tournaments (PRESET COMPETITION): every World Cup from 1930 to 2026 and every Euro from 1960 to 2024, the 2021 Africa Cup of Nations, Gold Cup and Copa América, the 2022 Women's Euro, and the European Cup and Champions Leagues of 1991-96 -- Insane's, from SWOS 2020.
  • Flags beside the team names on the score banner (PC, Mac, Linux).
  • Team packs: teams a mod brings, picked with TEAM PACKS on CHOOSE TEAMS.
  • The Amiga plays the mods too: MODES, PRACTICE, SPEEDRUN, CHALLENGES and the historic tournaments, with your settings and records kept in save/.
  • Amiga in NTSC: started from an NTSC Workbench, the game now fills the screen and runs at 50 Hz, and Workbench gets its own mode back when you quit.
  • Controllers: hold Start or R3 for a moment for ESC; a tap of Start still pauses.
  • Amiga: the mods' screens are now part of the program and their data is converted for the Amiga by OpenSWOS Setup, so the Amiga reads no JSON for them and their screens open at once. Run OpenSWOS Setup's Amiga step again to update your drawer or WHDLoad package: this SWOS2 does not read the mods an older setup put there. Speedrun records and challenge progress from an earlier beta start again.
  • Amiga careers load much faster (petarku's work): a big career took over four minutes to load on an A1200, now under twenty seconds. Saves are unchanged.
  • Old careers come across (petarku's work, SWOS 2020 added): a career saved by the original Amiga SWOS, the PC game or SWOS 2020 is rebuilt as an OpenSWOS career -- the squad, the money, the manager, the record and the finished seasons; the season in progress starts again. For now from the command line (the PC builds): SWOS --career-golden=rebuild:OLD.CAR --golden-seasons=0 --golden-out=FOLDER writes FOLDER/REBUILT.CAR, which LOAD opens. Please try your old careers and tell us what does not come across.
  • Amiga careers save much faster (petarku's work): about 16 seconds on an A1200 is now under 5. The save files are the same as before.
  • OpenSWOS Setup takes every PC version, not only GOG's: the CD version (its CD in the drive, a copy of it, or an image -- .iso, .img, .bin/.cue, or a .zip of one) and an installed floppy version. An image of the CD is the whole game on its own, commentary and all. An installed CD version leaves its commentary on the CD: the setup now looks in your CD drives for it, and asks for the CD if it is not there. The PC screen links two CD downloads (archive.org's and files.swos.eu's). The floppy version has no commentary; the 96/97 floppy upgrade disks alone are not a whole game, and the setup says so.
  • Challenges can now start from a corner, a free kick, a penalty, a throw-in or a goal kick, play golden or silver goal, be the second leg of a tie, and start with players injured. In a shoot-out challenge you are the keeper too.
  • Fixed: a career that was in a European cup could crash at the next season change after it was loaded; ESC out of a mod's match no longer quits the game; the cursor no longer sticks on a mod's screen after you back out of a team screen; on the main menu, down from LOAD OLD COMPETITION goes to MODES; an Amiga save that was too big no longer fails without a word; the score in a replay no longer shivers as the pitch scrolls; against a team in purple, the HALF TIME and FULL TIME banners, the corner flags and the red card no longer turn purple too; an old copy of the game's menus left in the assets folder no longer replaces the new ones (it hid the MODS row in OPTIONS and MODES on the main menu); OpenSWOS-Setup.zip has the mods in it again, so its Mods screen lists them; SWOS.ini no longer gets enabled = ?? under [mods]; the MODS screen scrolls (the arrows on its right), so a ninth mod, WEATHER, is on it again; on the Amiga (and a 4:3 PC) MODES and CREDITS no longer cover LOAD OLD COMPETITION when a career is loaded; a career's pre-season tours are told again -- the history screen showed them as cups, and their results never counted with the board; in a whole-match replay (the highlights viewer, and the online recordings player) the score is no longer drawn over the one before, which made a 2-0 unreadable; NEW CHALLENGE's SAVE replaced the new copy of a challenge instead of the old one; a challenge pack that is switched off keeps its progress; SPEEDRUN's TACKLE counts as SWOS 2020's does -- a goal with your tackle the last thing before it; in SHOOTING PRACTICE, the ball going out for a throw-in no longer stops the drill or crashes the game (the CPU's lone keeper was sent to take it): you take it.

What to test in this build

Every platform

  1. The picture (new renderer): start the game, play a match, go fullscreen and back (Alt+Enter), resize the window. Anything black, stretched, flickering or slow?
  2. RETRO TV: turn it on in OPTIONS → MODS, restart, try its settings on its page. F5 in the menus: PC and Amiga look.
  3. Online chat: in an online match, TAB, type, Enter; F3 hides it.
  4. The mouse in the menus, and OPTIONS → LANGUAGE: does every screen read right, and does every label fit its button?
  5. MODES on the main menu: PRACTICE (penalty shootout, demo game, team training, shooting practice), SPEEDRUN and CHALLENGES. Play one of each; leave a screen with EXIT and back out of a match with ESC.
  6. Historic tournaments: PRESET COMPETITION → HISTORIC TOURNAMENTS. Try EURO, WORLD CUP, CONTINENTAL CUPS and CLUB; start one and play a few matches of it. Are the teams and the groups right for that year?
  7. Flags (PC, Mac, Linux): a national team's flag beside its name on the score banner at kick-off, after a goal and at half and full time. A club shows its country's.
  8. OPTIONS → MODS (PC, Mac, Linux): turn a mod off, restart, and check it has gone; turn it back on.
  9. OpenSWOS Setup with the CD version: give it your CD (in the drive), a copy of it or an image (or one of the two CD downloads on its PC screen) and install. Does the game have the PC commentary (with Sound set to the PC game's in the setup's game options)? With an installed CD or floppy version, does it find the rest or say what is missing?
  10. CHALLENGES: play one that starts from a set piece (a corner, a free kick, a penalty) and one in extra time.

Amiga (a real A1200, FS-UAE or WinUAE)

  1. On a PC or Mac, run OpenSWOS Setup with your disk images and the PC game, then Amiga version → Add the game data to SWOS-Amiga.zip's drawer. Leave the mods on.
  2. Copy the drawer to your Amiga and start it from Workbench. Is MODES on the main menu? Try PRACTICE, SPEEDRUN, CHALLENGES and HISTORIC TOURNAMENTS as above. A mod's screen takes about a second the first time it opens, and a historic tournament two to five seconds to load: tell us if anything takes much longer.
  3. Do the same with the WHDLoad package the setup makes.
  4. Play a SPEEDRUN target, quit the game, start it again: is your best time still there?
  5. If your Workbench is in NTSC: the game should now fill the screen and run at 50 Hz, and Workbench should come back in its own mode when you quit.
